BOOKING FEE DEPOSIT

MOD's twenty-five percent (25%) booking fee deposit - which serves as Consultant’s Fee - can only be paid through a deposit to the authorized Hawthorne Passbook-Access only Peso Savings Account (3731027071). Deposits can be made Over the Counter, via online banking or fund transfer, but it has to be deposited to this bank account number only. Booking fee payments through other bank accounts or payment channels will not be accepted. Cash and/or Cheque booking fees paid in person will not be accepted, it has to be deposited to this authorized Hawthorne bank account only. However, the remaining seventy-five (75%) balance payment is paid directly to the client-companys' GCASH account, namely EXEMPLARY CLEANING SERVICES.
